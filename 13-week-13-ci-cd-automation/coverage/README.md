## Why the total is ~50 %, not 100 %

The uncovered lines are the **plugin-backed data layers** that need a real device
or backend and are deliberately replaced by fakes in tests:

- `lib/features/auth/data/api_client.dart`, `token_store.dart`
  (Dio + `flutter_secure_storage`),
- `lib/features/announcement/data/repositories/announcement_repository_impl.dart`,
  `models/announcement_model.dart`, `local/announcement_cache.dart`
  (the mock source the widget tests swap for a fake),
- `lib/core/messaging/push_service.dart`, `lib/main.dart`
  (Firebase Cloud Messaging and app bootstrap).

Coverage is a clue, not a goal. The business rules and the UI wiring that users
actually depend on are covered; the untested lines are the ones that require an
emulator to reach.
